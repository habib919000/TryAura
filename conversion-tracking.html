Have Questions?

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about conversion tracking.

What is Server-Side Tracking (CAPI)?

Server-Side Tracking sends data directly from your WooCommerce server to platforms like Facebook or Google, bypassing browser restrictions and ad blockers for better accuracy.

Does this work with the iOS 14+ update?

Yes! Our server-side tracking and advanced matching technology help recover data lost due to iOS 14 privacy changes, ensuring your attribution remains accurate.

Which platforms do you support?

We support all major platforms including Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, and more.

Do I need to be a developer to set this up?

No — our one-click setup means you just need to paste your Pixel IDs. We handle all the complex event tracking and server-side logic for you.
