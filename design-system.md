# TryAura Design System

## Colors

- **Black**: `#0E0E0F` (Text, primary buttons, navigation)
- **Yellow**: `#FFD932` (Primary accent, highlights, CTA buttons)
- **Gray Text**: `#575757` (Secondary text, paragraphs)
- **Gray Small**: `#828282` (Footer links, metadata)
- **Gray Light**: `#E9E9E9` (Borders, dividers)
- **Gray BG**: `#F6F6F6` (Section backgrounds, alternate cards)
- **Warm BG**: `#FFF8F0` (Workflow section background)

## Typography

- **Font Family**: 'Poppins', sans-serif
- **Heading 1**: 62px, 400 weight, 1.25 line-height
- **Heading 2**: 52px, 400 weight, 1.2 line-height
- **Heading 3**: 36px/24px, 400 weight, 1.2/1.3 line-height
- **Body Text**: 20px/18px/16px, 1.5 line-height

## Components

- **Primary Button**: Rounded 5px, Yellow background, Black text.
- **Secondary Button (Account)**: Rounded 5px, Black background, White text.
- **Tab Buttons**: Rounded corners, White/Black toggle, 18px Poppins.
- **FAQ Items**: Bordered cards with toggleable answers and plus/minus icons.
- **Feature Cards**: Bordered grid items with large imagery and descriptive text.
