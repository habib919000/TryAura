Have Questions?

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions by our users.

What is TryAura and how does it work with WooCommerce?

TryAura is a WooCommerce plugin that lets you generate AI-powered product visuals and virtual try-on experiences directly from your store's product editor.

How does virtual try-on help increase conversions?

Virtual try-on helps shoppers see how a product looks on them before buying. This improves product visualization, reduces hesitation, and helps customers make confident purchase decisions.

Do I need professional product images to use TryAura?

No — TryAura can work from basic product images and enhance them into professional-quality visuals.

Can I create both product images and videos with TryAura?

Yes — TryAura supports both studio-quality product images and short AI-generated product videos.

Is TryAura suitable for all types of WooCommerce stores?

TryAura is designed for any WooCommerce store selling physical products, especially clothing, accessories, and lifestyle items.

What does "Bring Your Own Key" mean?

It means you connect your own Google Gemini API key. This gives you full control over usage and eliminates per-image fees charged by other platforms.

Is the Free plan really unlimited?

Yes — with your own Gemini key, you can generate unlimited images and try-ons. You only pay Google's API rate, not TryAura per-generation fees.

Will TryAura slow down my WooCommerce store?

No — TryAura is built with performance in mind and only loads assets when needed, so it won't impact your store's page speed.
